# Job Search Playbook: what the data says to do

Written 2026-07 after 39 sales applications produced zero interviews (21
application-stage rejections, 18 unanswered). The targeting config was retuned
at the same time (see config/rubric.md, "winnable-first retune"). The config
fixes what gets applied to; this playbook fixes how.

## 1. Reply to the stalled recruiter threads first

All five live Recruiter Screen rows in the Pipeline are inbound recruiters
waiting on a reply from Chase, not the other side. They are the only human
responses in the entire pipeline. Clear that backlog before sending a single
new application. (The weekly review of Jul 13-20 flagged the same thing.)

## 2. Finalize and file the sales resume

The sales resume exists only as a Gmail attachment ("Chase Vandiver Resume
Sales.pdf", sent to Jeff 7/16). Two edits are agreed and pending: drop the
"Now focused on..." summary line, and add an AI-capability line under core
skills/tools. Apply them, export the PDF, and save the canonical copy to
Google Drive so `Chase_Vandiver_Resume_Sales.pdf` in config/match-profile.json
points at a real, current file.

The two roles Chase himself flagged as best-fit (DataRails Partnership
Manager: "the exact job"; HubSpot SMB AE: "built as a first AE seat") both
died at resume screen. When the targets are right and the screen still fails,
the artifact is the problem. Every bullet on the sales resume should map to
one of the evidenceAnchors in match-profile.json, led by the $300K
zero-to-recurring sponsorship program.

## 3. Referral-first on every A-tier role

All 18 open applications went in cold. For every A-tier role, make one warm
touch before or alongside the application: a LinkedIn message to the hiring
manager or a current AE/partnerships person, a mutual contact, or an SMU
alumni search at the company. Cold-apply-only is how a pivot resume loses to
the ATS filter every time.

## 4. A-tier means apply within 48 hours

Six of twelve A-tier radar rows expired before an application went out. The
freshness gate already skips anything older than 14 days; the same urgency
applies on the outbound side. When a role hits A, it gets applied to (with its
warm touch) within 48 hours or it gets consciously passed with a note.

## 5. Backfill the Final-Round Log

The log has one placeholder row referencing "eight finals and no offer" from
the pre-pivot search. That pattern is worth more than any new application:
backfill the three most recent finals (company, stage reached, stated reason,
where it broke down, lesson) so the next final round is played differently.

## 6. Watch the Response stat, not the application count

The dashboard now shows a Response tile: the percent of pipeline rows that
ever advanced past Applied. That number, not applications sent, is the
measure of whether targeting and materials are working. If it stays near zero
after the next 15 winnable-first applications, the next suspect is the resume
content, and after that the positioning itself. Feed what you learn back into
config/match-profile.json.
